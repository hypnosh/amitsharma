Product Market Fit becomes easier to reason about when broad conviction is translated
into specific questions: who the audience is, what problem matters, what customers do
today, where the economic value comes from, why they would pay, and which assumptions
could invalidate the idea.

> Is there a specific customer who needs this badly enough to pay for it?

This is a working document. The frameworks establish a structure; the exercises
challenge your assumptions; the worksheets make you apply the thinking to your own
product. Customer discovery and validation tools help you distinguish belief from
evidence. The final output is a Product Market Fit hypothesis you can actually test.

> You don't need to be looking for Product Market Fit from scratch. You just need to be
> willing to question what you think you know.
