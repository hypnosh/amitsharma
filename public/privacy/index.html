Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Amit Sharma (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit www.amitsharma.tech or use the PMF Evaluator and related services.

The PMF Evaluator is a free interactive tool that helps founders think through their product opportunity, identify uncertainties and determine what to investigate next.

By using our website or submitting information through the PMF Evaluator, you acknowledge that your information will be processed as described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our website and tools, we may collect the following information.

Information you provide

When you use the PMF Evaluator, we may collect:

  • Email address: Required to access your personalised PMF Opportunity Map.
  • Product and business information: Your product or idea description, intended audience, problem statement and business context.
  • Founder information: Information you provide about your relevant experience, expertise, capabilities or access to potential customers.
  • Assessment responses: Your selected business stage, responses about customer evidence and any uncertainty you identify.
  • Communications: Information you provide if you contact us, submit feedback or communicate with us directly.

Please avoid submitting confidential client information, sensitive personal information, financial account details, trade secrets or other information that is not necessary to assess your product opportunity.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website or use our tools, certain technical information may be collected, depending on the services and analytics configured. This may include:

  • Browser and device information.
  • IP address and technical request information.
  • Pages visited and interactions with the evaluator.
  • Referral sources and campaign attribution.
  • Assessment completion and error events.

We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate, secure and improve our services.

3. How we use your information

We use the information collected for purposes including:

  • Generating your personalised PMF Opportunity Map.
  • Processing and recording your evaluator submission.
  • Maintaining the functionality, reliability and security of the website and evaluator.
  • Diagnosing errors and improving the quality of AI-generated responses.
  • Understanding aggregate usage and conversion patterns.
  • Responding to enquiries and feedback.
  • Sending product updates, educational content or relevant offers where you have provided the required consent.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations and addressing legitimate security or abuse concerns.

We do not use your answers to automatically certify product-market fit or determine whether your business will succeed.

4. AI processing and Google Gemini

The PMF Evaluator uses Google’s Gemini API to generate a personalised assessment based on the information you submit.

To provide this functionality, relevant assessment answers and instructions may be transmitted to Google’s services for processing. The information may include your product description, target audience, founder stage, reported evidence and stated business uncertainties.

AI-generated outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate. They are intended to help you reason about your opportunity and are not independent verification of your claims, professional advice or a prediction of business success.

Your assessment may also be logged for debugging, reliability monitoring and output-quality improvement. Where we retain such logs, they may include the prompt, model response, processed output, model configuration, timing and error information.

We aim to minimise personal information in these logs and restrict access to them. Please do not submit sensitive or confidential information that is unnecessary for the assessment.

Google processes information in accordance with its applicable terms and policies. Google’s handling of information depends on the specific Gemini API service, account configuration and applicable terms. Refer to Google’s relevant documentation for details.

5. Where your information is stored and processed

We use third-party technology services to operate the website and evaluator. These currently include, as applicable to the deployed service:

  • Hugo and Cloudflare Pages: To serve the website.
  • Cloudflare Workers: To process evaluator submissions and make server-side API requests.
  • Google Forms and Google Sheets: To collect and store submitted assessment and lead information.
  • Google Gemini API: To generate AI-assisted assessment results.

These services may process or store information on infrastructure located in different jurisdictions, depending on their configurations and applicable service arrangements.

We do not control the internal infrastructure or independent privacy practices of these providers. Their processing is governed by their respective terms, policies and contractual arrangements.

We do not intentionally make your assessment publicly accessible. Access to stored submissions and diagnostic logs should be restricted to authorised persons who need it for the purposes described in this policy.

6. Email communications and marketing

Your email address is required to access the PMF Evaluator’s output.

Providing your email address for this purpose does not, by itself, mean that you have consented to receive promotional emails.

Where we offer marketing or newsletter subscriptions, we will seek any separate consent required by applicable law. You may withdraw your marketing consent or unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time.

We may send communications necessary to fulfil a request you have made or respond to an enquiry, where permitted by applicable law.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share or permit processing of information in limited circumstances:

  • With service providers that help operate the website, store submissions or generate assessments.
  • Where necessary to protect the security and integrity of our services.
  • Where required by applicable law, a valid legal process or a competent authority.
  • Where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • In connection with a business transfer, where applicable law permits and appropriate safeguards are maintained.

We seek to limit information shared with service providers to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information for only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods. For example, lead records, assessment responses, AI diagnostic logs and security records may have different operational requirements.

Where information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise dispose of it securely, subject to applicable legal obligations and technical limitations.

You may contact us to request deletion of your information, as described below.

9. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

These measures may include restricting access to stored records, protecting API credentials, using server-side processing for AI requests and limiting access to diagnostic logs.

However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot promise absolute security.

Please do not submit information that you would not be comfortable sharing with the service providers necessary to operate the evaluator.

10. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information associated with you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Opt out of promotional communications.
  • Raise a concern or submit a privacy-related complaint.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

11. Cookies and analytics

Our website or its service providers may use cookies, similar technologies or analytics mechanisms to understand website usage, measure the evaluator funnel, diagnose technical issues and improve performance.

The technologies used depend on the site’s current configuration. We aim to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information through analytics and do not intend to send email addresses or free-text business descriptions to analytics platforms.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

Where required by applicable law, we will seek consent before using technologies that require it.

12. Children’s privacy

The PMF Evaluator is intended for founders and other adults evaluating business opportunities. It is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect children’s personal information through the evaluator.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

13. International processing

Some of our service providers may process information outside India. Where this occurs, we will address applicable legal requirements governing cross-border processing and take reasonable steps to protect the information.

The countries in which information is processed may depend on the providers and their service configurations.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the evaluator, the technologies we use, our data-handling practices or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date. Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or seek consent for material changes.
