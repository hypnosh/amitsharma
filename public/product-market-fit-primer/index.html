The problem

Building the product is only half the job.

  1. You know your industry.
  2. You understand the problem.
  3. You know what you can build.

But none of that answers the question that matters:

Is there a specific customer who needs this badly enough to pay for it?

And that's not just a sales and marketing question. It's a product question.

The framework

Four things need to come together.

The intersection is where a Product Market Fit opportunity begins.

The book's promise

The Primer helps you work through the questions before you spend more time and money building.

  1. Who exactly is the audience?
  2. What problem are you actually solving?
  3. How important is that problem?
  4. What are customers doing today?
  5. Where does the economic value come from?
  6. Why would they pay?
  7. What advantage do you have?
  8. Which assumptions could invalidate the idea?
  9. What do you need to find out next?

Built to be worked through, not just read.

Product Market Fit becomes easier to reason about when broad conviction is translated into specific questions: who the audience is, what problem matters, what customers do today, where the economic value comes from, why they would pay, and which assumptions could invalidate the idea.

Is there a specific customer who needs this badly enough to pay for it?

This is a working document. The frameworks establish a structure; the exercises challenge your assumptions; the worksheets make you apply the thinking to your own product. Customer discovery and validation tools help you distinguish belief from evidence. The final output is a Product Market Fit hypothesis you can actually test.

You don’t need to be looking for Product Market Fit from scratch. You just need to be willing to question what you think you know.

Who it's for

This is for you if...

You're a founder who:

  • has a product or product idea
  • understands a particular industry or customer
  • can build, or has a team that can
  • is pre-revenue or early revenue
  • is entering a new market or launching something new
  • isn't completely sure you've found the right buyer

Particularly relevant to founders building in fintech, insurtech, wealthtech, SaaS and other complex or regulated markets.

You don't need to be looking for Product Market Fit from scratch. You just need to be willing to question what you think you know.

Who it's not for

Probably not for you if...

You're looking for:

  • a startup idea
  • someone to tell you what to build
  • a generic business-plan template
  • motivational startup content
  • a shortcut around talking to customers
If you already have strong, repeatable evidence that customers want and pay for your product, your next problem is probably somewhere else.
